A few useful answers.

Is DaysOff free?

Yes. You can plan your leave without creating an account or starting a subscription.

Can I use my own holidays?

Yes. Add your own days off and important dates, including repeating events. Automatic country and regional holiday data is optional.

Will my plan appear on another device?

Your plan is stored in the browser where you create it. It does not automatically sync to other browsers or devices. Clearing that browser’s site data removes the saved plan.

Does it replace my employer’s leave system?

DaysOff helps you plan. It does not submit leave requests or confirm approval. Check your allowance, holidays and leave arrangements with your employer.
