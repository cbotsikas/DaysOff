# DaysOff

[DaysOff](https://cbotsikas.github.io/DaysOff/) is a private calendar and time-off planner that helps people organise their year and make better use of their leave. It brings working schedules, planned leave, holidays, school closures, and important dates into one calendar.

Its purpose is to answer practical questions: When am I free? How much leave have I planned? Where can a few leave days create a longer break?

## Main features

- Calendar views: month cards, date rows, and weekday-aligned rows on desktop; month cards on mobile. Printing follows the selected layout.
- Personal leave: select individual dates or ranges, edit existing ranges, and organise leave into custom types with colours and symbols.
- Annual allowances: optionally set available days for each leave type and year, see the remaining balance, and identify over-allocation.
- Flexible working schedules: configure a weekly pattern, alternating weeks, or a repeating shift cycle. Leave totals follow the actual working schedule.
- Holidays and custom days off: use optional country/subdivision holiday data or create personal dates, and decide which ones count as days off.
- Important events: mark birthdays, deadlines, company events, and date ranges without consuming leave. Recurrence options include yearly dates, weekday-based rules, and dates relative to Western or Orthodox Easter.
- School closures: add named dates or ranges and copy previous-year entries. These provide planning context without changing leave calculations.
- Longer-break summaries: see consecutive free periods created by the combination of schedules, holidays, and personal leave.
- Convenient interaction: desktop range selection, contextual actions, mobile date details, keyboard navigation, and session undo/redo.
- Personalisation: English, German, and Greek; configurable date formats; light, dark, and system appearance.

## Privacy and availability

DaysOff works without an account. Plans and settings are stored in the current browser, and the app works offline after its first visit. It can also be installed as a PWA on a phone or computer.
